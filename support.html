Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a sound not playing?

Open the Parents page and make sure Sound Effects and Letter Voice are enabled. Also confirm that your device volume is turned up and Silent Mode is not preventing audio.

How is learning progress saved?

Completed letters are saved locally on the device. Removing the app may remove locally stored progress.

How do I remove advertisements?

Open the Parents page and choose Remove Ads. This is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Parents page and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID that made the original purchase.

What should I do if tracing does not complete?

Start near the numbered point, follow the arrow direction, and finish near the end of the guide. Use the reset button to try the letter again.

How can I report a problem?

Email us with the letter or activity involved and include a screenshot when possible.
